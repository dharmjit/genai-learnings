0. THE COST OF KEEPING THREADS RESIDENT
   registers / SM           65536 (32-bit) = 256 KiB
   registers / thread        42.7 at full occupancy
   register file, whole GPU  47.0 MiB across 188 SMs
   --> this is where the transistor budget went: thread state,
       not per-thread cleverness.

5. MEMORY LATENCY, AND LITTLE'S LAW
   idle round-trip latency     158.9 ns  (386 cycles at 2.43 GHz)
   throughput = concurrency / latency, so to sustain 1532 GB/s:
     1532 GB/s x 159 ns = 243 KB in flight
     at 16 B per thread that is only ~15213 threads

   But experiment 4 needed far more than that. Invert the law on the
   measured curve to see the latency each row ACTUALLY experienced:
        threads         GB/s in flight (KB)  implied lat
          12032        466.5            193        413 ns
          48128       1417.4            770        543 ns
          96256       1516.8           1540       1015 ns
         192512       1528.0           3080       2016 ns
         385024       1523.6           6160       4043 ns
         770048       1532.2          12321       8041 ns
   --> latency is not a constant. It degrades as you approach
       saturation, so you need more threads than the idle number
       suggests. Little's Law gives a FLOOR, not a target.
